Byte-pair encoding: the merge process

BPE starts with each word split into characters plus an end-of-word marker _. Each step counts every adjacent pair of tokens across the corpus (weighted by word frequency), merges the most frequent pair into a new token everywhere it occurs, and appends that merge to the merge list. The pair that will be merged next is outlined; tokens created by the last merge are highlighted. See also the same process on a trie.

    Ties between equally frequent pairs are broken by taking the alphabetically first pair, so results can differ slightly from implementations that break ties by order of occurrence. Merging scans each word left to right, so in a a a the pair (a, a) merges once, giving aa a.
